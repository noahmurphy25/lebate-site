Privacy Policy

The LeBate App · Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version

The LeBate App does not collect your personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your device

  • The username you pick
  • Your level, XP, quiz and exam scores, streak and settings

This information is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it, and you can erase it any time with "Reset my progress" in the Locker tab.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads in this version.
  • We do not sell or share information, because we do not collect any.

Links out of the app

The Give tab opens the LeBron James Family Foundation website in your browser. We are not affiliated with the foundation, we do not process donations, and we never see any donation details. Once you leave the app, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, leaderboards or ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will tell you what changes.
