Common questions

How do I reach Level 10?
Pass all 40 course quizzes (80%), all 8 semester exams (90%), all 4 Jordan Slander Pop Quizzes (100%), reach 8,600 XP, then pass the 50-question final with 95%.
Why do I need 8,600 XP for the final?
Passing everything once earns 8,000. The rest comes from perfect scores, so the final is reserved for people who aced some of it.
I failed a quiz and can't retake it.
There's a 3-minute wait after a failed quiz, 30 minutes after a semester exam and 24 hours after the final. Use the time to reread the study guide.
Is my progress saved if I get a new phone?
Not yet. Version 1 saves progress on your device only. Accounts and leaderboards are coming in an update.
How do I get my diploma?
Pass the final. The diploma is a printable PDF with your username, and it's free. You'll find it on the graduation screen, on Home and in the Locker tab.
How do I erase my progress?
Locker tab, "Reset my progress." Deleting the app also erases everything.
A stat looks wrong.
Email the course name and question, and it'll be fixed in the next update. Stats are current through the season noted in the app.
